🛠️ Personal Gmail Automation Guide

How Satnam can automate 2-day advance emails using his personal Gmail without paying for servers:

Step 1: Open Google Apps Script

Go to script.google.com with your Google Account and click New Project.

Step 2: Copy the Automation Code

Replace the script with the provided code in automation/Code.gs from your project. It connects to your Firebase RTDB and uses GmailApp.sendEmail().

Step 3: Create Daily Trigger

In Apps Script, click the clock icon (Triggers) → Add Trigger:
• Choose sendDailyReminders
• Select Time-driven → Day timer → 8am to 9am.
Every Monday at 8 AM, it reminds who is on Garbage Day this Wednesday (2 days before)!
Every Friday at 8 AM, it reminds who is on Cleaning Duty this Sunday!
